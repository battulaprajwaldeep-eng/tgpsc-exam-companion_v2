How coverage is classified

Listed: appears in the official syllabus transcription.

Needs question development: no live-bank question is mapped to the subtopic.

Learning content: this audit does not count a heading as teaching material. Detailed notes, worked examples and verified exam-level question sets must be authored and reviewed separately before a subtopic can be declared learning-complete.
