3 · Listening tips

Voice quality and available Indian-English voices depend on your Android device and browser. Choose an English (India) voice if one is available. This feature generates speech on your device; it does not create or download an MP3.

Long scripts are split into smaller sections to reduce playback interruptions. Keep this page open; some phones pause speech when the screen locks or the browser goes into the background. Start playback before your journey and do not operate the phone while riding.

Your lesson text is saved in this browser only. It is not uploaded to an account.
